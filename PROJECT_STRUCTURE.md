# Intellectual Lineage

The architecture evolved through the following progression:

```text
Decision Proof
    ↓
Mutation Governance
    ↓
Authority Continuity
    ↓
Legitimacy Emergence
    ↓
Epistemic Boundaries
    ↓
Continuity of Truth
```

---

# Central Question

The project began with:

"How do we stop the ghost?"

It evolved into:

"How do we force the ghost to leave evidence?"

---

# Final Invariant

Truth may fail.

Execution may fail.

Authority may fail.

Governance may fail.

Evidence must survive.

Continuity must remain recoverable.
