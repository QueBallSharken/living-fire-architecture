# Living Mycelium Architecture (LMA)

> Truth survives even when execution does not.

Living Mycelium Architecture (LMA) is a continuity-first distributed systems architecture inspired by biological mycelium networks.

Rather than assuming perfect trust, permanent authority, or flawless security, LMA assumes:

- Compromise is inevitable.
- Failure is inevitable.
- Uncertainty is inevitable.
- Recovery must be possible.
- Evidence must survive.

The objective is not to eliminate every attack.

The objective is to ensure that attacks, failures, contradictions, and unknown conditions cannot destroy the system's ability to understand what happened, preserve continuity, and recover with integrity.

---

# Core Philosophy

Traditional systems ask:

- Who are you?
- What are you allowed to do?
- Did you authenticate?

LMA asks:

- Was authority legitimate?
- Was governance present at the point of effect?
- Can continuity survive authority loss?
- Can truth survive execution failure?
- Can compromise strengthen the architecture?

The architecture treats security as a survivability problem rather than a prevention problem.

---

# Foundational Assumptions

LMA assumes that every system eventually encounters:

- Component failure
- Software defects
- Operator mistakes
- Supply chain compromise
- Governance failure
- Authority loss
- Unknown attack classes
- Ontological uncertainty

The architecture is designed to continue operating when possible and fail with integrity when necessary.

---

# The Mycelium Principle

A mycelium network survives because no single strand is the network.

Individual paths may die.

The network persists through distributed continuity.

Likewise:

- A node may fail.
- A region may fail.
- An authority may disappear.
- A governance structure may fracture.

As long as evidence survives, continuity can be rebuilt.

---

# Core Invariant

The central invariant of LMA is:

> Continuity of Truth is more important than Continuity of Execution.

Execution may stop.

Authority may disappear.

Governance may fail.

Truth must remain recoverable.

# Architectural Layers

## Layer 0 — Witness Mesh

Independent observers.

Responsibilities:

- Attestation
- Observation
- Contradiction detection
- Evidence preservation

Purpose:

No single actor should be capable of rewriting reality.

---

## Layer 1 — Governance Engine

Responsible for:

- Policy enforcement
- Rate limiting
- Quorum evaluation
- Recovery coordination
- Invariant verification

Purpose:

Ensure governance exists where decisions become actions.

---

## Layer 2 — Regional Deployments

Distributed execution environments.

Purpose:

Prevent geographic, political, technical, or operational monoculture.

Regions operate independently while preserving continuity.

---

## Layer 3 — Verified Handoff Protocol (VHP)

Cryptographically verifiable transfer of state between systems.

Purpose:

Ensure continuity survives migration, recovery, failover, and succession.

---

## Layer 4 — Quantum Skin

A supplementary observation layer.

Purpose:

Detect anomalies, contradictions, and state deviations through independent sensing mechanisms.

The Quantum Skin is a witness.

It is not an authority.

# Design Principles

## Evidence Over Assumption

Claims require evidence.

Evidence survives claims.

---

## Polyculture Over Monoculture

Diversity increases survivability.

No implementation should become a single point of systemic failure.

---

## Attestation Over Trust

Trust is temporary.

Evidence is durable.

---

## Containment Over Prevention

Compromise is expected.

Blast radius must remain bounded.

---

## Recovery Over Perfection

Systems should be recoverable rather than assumed infallible.

---

## Adaptive Resilience

Every failure should generate knowledge.

Every contradiction should improve observability.

Every attack should increase future attack cost.

---

# Threat Model

LMA assumes attackers may compromise:

- Software
- Hardware
- Networks
- Governance
- Operators
- Identity systems
- Supply chains

The objective is not to guarantee prevention.

The objective is to preserve:

- Integrity
- Evidence
- Continuity
- Recoverability

under adverse conditions.

---

# Failure Philosophy

A system that continues operating while corrupted may generate unlimited damage.

A system that halts with integrity preserves truth.

LMA therefore recognizes two successful outcomes:

## Outcome A

Execution survives.

Continuity continues.

---

## Outcome B

Execution halts.

Evidence survives.

Continuity can be rebuilt.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Living Mycelium Architecture explores how distributed systems can:

- Survive compromise
- Preserve truth
- Learn from contradiction
- Adapt without losing integrity
- Recover after authority loss
- Maintain continuity across uncertainty

The goal is not immortality.

The goal is survivability.

The goal is continuity.

The goal is truth.
